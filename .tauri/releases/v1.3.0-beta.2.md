## Boris 1.3.0-beta.2

Second beta of Boris 1.3 for Windows 10 and 11 x64. This beta is about getting
a fresh install to answer "Hey Boris" and saying clearly why when it can't.
Stable installations stay on 1.2.0 unless you select the Beta update channel.
NSIS installer only.

### What's new

- A first-run welcome flow connects your OpenRouter key, lets you choose fast
  replies or a lighter PC, picks a microphone, then downloads the voice models.
  New installs start listening as soon as setup finishes.
- An on-device setup check runs "Hey Boris, what time is it?" through the real
  wake, speech-detection, and speech-to-text models, then checks the live mic
  and speakers. It runs the first time Boris listens and can be rerun from
  Home. "Repair voice models" re-downloads any damaged model files.
- The OpenRouter key check says whether your key works, has no credit left,
  was rejected, or could not be reached, in setup and in Connections settings.
- A soft chime plays the moment "Hey Boris" is accepted, so you know when to
  speak. Turn it off in Voice & Audio settings.
- A Privacy settings page, and a "Show advanced settings" toggle that hides
  developer-level controls by default.

### Reliability

- Boris says when the microphone delivers only silence (Windows mic privacy,
  mute key, dead input), with a button that opens the Windows microphone
  privacy page, and when a wake was ignored by the speaker or taught-voice
  filter. These show even with every window hidden.
- Unplugging or switching a microphone or speaker no longer needs a restart.
  Boris follows the Windows default and picks your device back up when it is
  plugged in again.
- Choosing "Quit Boris" from the tray while Boris was running no longer
  freezes the app.
- A missing or too-old `onnxruntime.dll` no longer leaves Boris stuck at
  "Starting…". The installer now ships the Visual C++ runtime the speech engine
  needs, and Boris explains what is missing if it still can't load.
- Changing the key, model, barge-in, wake chime, or "Ignore TVs and videos"
  takes effect right away instead of after Stop/Start.
- Device switches and setup checks during a turn no longer cancel a pending
  yes/no confirmation.

### Install and update

1. Download `Boris_1.3.0-beta.2_x64-setup.exe` and run it over your existing Boris installation.
2. Select **Settings > General > Updates > Beta** to receive future beta updates.
3. On a first installation, the welcome flow walks you through your OpenRouter
   key, microphone, and model download.

Your `%USERPROFILE%\.boris` data stays outside the installation directory.
Existing installs skip the welcome flow and keep their settings and memory.

### Developer notes

- `StatusPicture` gains `listen_hint` and `self_check`. `boris-audio` exposes
  per-stream `AudioHealth` / `StreamHealth`.
- New config keys: `[ui] onboarding_complete`, `[ui] show_advanced_settings`,
  `[speech] wake_chime`. New env var: `BORIS_WAKE_CHIME`.
- `boris_sense::init_onnx_runtime` preflights the ONNX Runtime library before
  `ort` loads it, and `boris-desktop` pins `ORT_DYLIB_PATH` to the DLL beside
  the exe. See `CLAUDE.md` for why both must stay until `ort` fixes its
  failed-load deadlock.
